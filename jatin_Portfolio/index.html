Project Summary

BillBook Pro is an enterprise-grade Billing, Inventory, GST, and Business Management Web Application built using React.js and Node.js. It is engineered to streamline business operations for retailers, wholesalers, and commercial operations.

Featuring an intuitive POS terminal, automated tax filing computations, AI-driven business intelligence, WhatsApp commerce integration, and role-based staff permissions, BillBook Pro offers an all-in-one cloud command center for commercial growth.
